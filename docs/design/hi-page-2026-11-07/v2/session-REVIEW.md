# njct.io/hi round 2: hostile review (2026-11-08)

Coordinates are in screenshot pixels (2x, so 390 CSS px = 780 px wide). The site references are `scratchpad/site/phone.png`, `desk.png` and `phone-about.png`.

## e-sleeve

**Verdict:** the closest to the site's own phone look, with readable hierarchy. It is safe and not much fun, and its sun shows where the layout box sits.

1. **LINE glyph reads "LIN" (confirmed).** Seen in en.png at (80,1267) and in every language. The knock-out mask `#m-line` draws L, I and N, but the E was never drawn. Fix: replace the `<g fill="#000">` inside `#m-line` with `<rect x="3.6" y="6" width="1" height="4" rx=".3"/><rect x="3.6" y="9" width="2.2" height="1" rx=".3"/><rect x="6.4" y="6" width="1" height="4" rx=".3"/><path d="M8.1 6h1l1.5 2.2V6h1v4h-1L9.1 7.8V10h-1z"/><path d="M12.2 6h2.4v.9h-1.4v.65h1.3v.9h-1.3v.65h1.4v.9h-2.4z"/>`, which centres all four letters on x=9. *Mechanical, builder applies.*
2. **The sun shows where the layout box sits.** On en.png, the peach patch's left edge is a hard vertical line at x≈40, parallel to the screen edge and level with the text gutter. It reads as a panel, not as window light. On desk.png it starts exactly at the 430 px column's left edge (x≈885), and the left half of the wall gets nothing. The cause is `<polygon points="20,116 900,-113 900,527 20,756">` drawn inside `.page`. Fix: change the left jamb's x from 20 to -300 so the light enters from off-screen. At ≥600 px, set `.sun.wall { position: fixed; inset: 0; width: 100vw; height: 100vh; }` and size the shade mask from `getBoundingClientRect()`. *Mechanical, builder applies.*
3. **The leaf shadows look like chevrons.** On en.png at (540–780, 380–520), and across the cover's right side, the generated fronds read as rows of `»` arrows, a generated motif. The site's light carries tropical leaf shapes. Fix: raise `#leafblur` stdDeviation from 2.6 to 5, and make the leaflets wider (`w = 14 - 6*t`) so they read as fronds. *Mechanical, builder applies.*
4. **The cover's left edge does not line up with the text.** On en.png the sleeve starts at x≈72 while the heading and pills start at x≈33, so there are two left edges stacked on top of each other. The same happens on desk.png. Fix: remove `style="margin-inline:auto"` from `#sleeve`, set `.sleeve { margin: 18px 0 0; }`, and add `[dir=rtl] .sleeve { margin-inline-start: 0; }`, so the cover hangs from the text's left edge (the right edge in RTL). *Mechanical, builder applies.*
5. **The cover has a generic soft drop shadow.** On en.png at y≈780–800 there is a grey blur band under the sleeve (`0 18px 34px -22px rgba(0,0,0,.45)`), plus the blurred "shade" rectangle. Together they make the stock card shadow. The site's covers cast no blur below. Fix: delete the second `box-shadow` layer and keep `0 0 0 .5px`. The sun mask's shade alone gives the sleeve its depth. *Mechanical, builder applies.*
6. **The line below the greeting leaves a one-word orphan.** "find me." sits alone on its line in en.png, en-360.png and desk.png at y≈1040. Fix: in `.line`, replace `text-wrap: pretty; max-width: 32ch` with `text-wrap: balance; max-width: none`. *Mechanical, builder applies.*
7. **The UI type is 16 px. The site's is 14.** The wordmark, the picker and the pills render about 14% larger than on site/phone.png: the wordmark is 45 CSS px wide against the site's 40. Fix: set `body { font: 400 14px/20px var(--face) }` and `.pill { font-size: 14px }`, and keep the picker sheet at 16 px as the site does. *Mechanical, builder applies.*
8. **Dead band above the foot.** About 110 CSS px of empty wall between the pills and the rule (en.png y 1310–1530). Fix: once item 4 is applied, set `--s: min(358px, calc(100vw - 32px), 44dvh)` so the cover takes the room. *Design, operator decides.*
9. **Fun.** Once the sun has drifted in, nothing moves or responds. The page is the Neil cover with a contact form under it, and nothing on it is personal beyond the cover. *Design, operator decides.*

## f-back

**Verdict:** the type is faithful to the cover back (Fraunces, #4A3212, #B8862C), but the scene around it is the most generated-looking of the four: a centred card with a drop shadow, a blurred blob sun and a glossy marble.

1. **The sun is blur blobs.** On en.png at (420–780, 110–340) there are six skewed peach rectangles with `filter: blur(7px)` and no leaves. They read as out-of-focus cards, the "decorative blob" trope, and look nothing like the site's window light. Fix: delete `.sun i` and port e-sleeve's window mask (with its item 2 fix applied) as a fixed full-viewport layer. *Design, operator decides.*
2. **The sphere is a stock 3D marble, and too small to be the head.** At (605–703, 440–540) a 49 CSS px ball has a hot specular spot (`#FFE3A0` at 0). The cover's sphere is matte. Stage 2 needs the head here, and at 49 px it is an icon. Fix: drop the first two stops so the key starts at `#F0B654`. The head's size is a separate decision: either make it at least 30% of the card width and set the headline beside or under it, or leave the head on the front and keep the flip. *Size: design, operator decides. Hotspot: mechanical, builder applies.*
3. **The card is centred on the screen, with a generic soft shadow.** On en.png about 150 CSS px of empty wall sits above the card and about 200 below. `0 22px 36px -20px` gives the stock card shadow (y 1110–1150). Fix: set `.stage { justify-content: flex-start; padding-top: 16px }` and use `box-shadow: 0 0 0 .5px rgb(60 40 10 / .18)` only. *Mechanical, builder applies.*
4. **The wordmark appears a second time.** "njct.io" is printed again inside the card (en.png at 668,1066). The brief puts the wordmark once, top left. Fix: delete `<span class="mark">` and its CSS. *Mechanical, builder applies.*
5. **Save contact is bigger and bolder than any site pill.** It is 48 px tall, at least 212 px wide, 16 px/500, and centred (en.png y 1158–1252). The site's pills hug their text at 44 px and 14 px/400. Fix: `.save { height: 44px; min-width: 0; padding: 0 16px; border-radius: 22px; font-size: 14px; font-weight: 400; }`. *Mechanical, builder applies.*
6. **The line below the greeting is low contrast.** `#86704E` on `#F4EADA` is about 4.0:1 for 17 px text. Fix: in `.sub`, set `color: #6E5A3C` (about 5.6:1). The accent rules stay as they are. *Mechanical, builder applies.*
7. **Orphan.** "find me." sits alone in en.png and en-360.png. Fix: `.sub { text-wrap: balance }`. *Mechanical, builder applies.*
8. **The wordmark's tap target is 20 px tall.** `.wordmark` has no padding. Fix: `.wordmark { top: 3px; padding: 12px 0; min-width: 44px; }`, as the site sets under `pointer: coarse`. *Mechanical, builder applies.*
9. **Font weight on a QR load.** It loads eight families (Fraunces, Inter, Noto Sans Hebrew, Thai and SC, Noto Serif Hebrew, Thai and SC) with `display=block` and no `text=` subset, so the text can stay blank for up to 3 s on mobile data. Fix: split the request in two, as h-crate does: the non-Latin families with `&text=` built from COPY, and Fraunces with `&text=` built from the five greetings. *Mechanical, builder applies.*
10. **Fun.** The flip from front to back is the one fun moment, and it ends on a static list. *Design, operator decides.*

## g-about

**Verdict:** a faithful copy of the About page (band #EFE2CC, the header with its rule, large Inter type, ruled rows). The sphere is a stock-render hero ball and the page is the least fun of the four.

1. **The sphere is a stock 3D render.** At (296–702, 180–585) a large ball has a cream specular hotspot (`rgb(255 226 160 / .9)`) and a dark rim ring, with no letters or paper behind it. "Ball above headline" on a flat colour is the generic hero layout. The cover's sphere is matte satin under gold "Neil". Fix: remove the "broad low sheen" layer, and either restore the cover's paper square with the traced "Neil" (`covers/letters/about.json`) behind the ball or keep the ball alone. *Sheen: mechanical, builder applies. Cover square: design, operator decides.*
2. **The ball sits off to the right for no stated reason.** `right: clamp(4px, 6vw, 40px)` puts it right, and its shadow stretches about 180 CSS px to the left (en.png y 560–600). The empty left half of the top third does no job. Fix: either centre it (`right: calc(50% - var(--r))`) or hang it from the text's left edge. *Design, operator decides.*
3. **On a laptop, Save contact falls below the fold.** At 1280×800 (desk.png) the pill is cut at the bottom edge (y≈790). Fix: `@media (min-width: 900px) { .still { --d: min(236px, 19svh); } }`. *Mechanical, builder applies.*
4. **The wordmark's tap target is 20 px tall.** `.mark` has no padding. Fix: `.mark { padding: 12px 0; min-width: 44px; }`. *Mechanical, builder applies.*
5. **In Thai, Save contact touches the bottom edge.** On th.png the greeting wraps to two lines and the pill ends at y≈1680 of 1688. Fix: `:lang(th) .hi { font-size: clamp(34px, 11vw, 56px) }`. *Mechanical, builder applies.*
6. **The café light is missing.** The band has no sun, so the sundial motion lights the ball but nothing else in the room. It is consistent with the About page, but it drops the one trace of warmth the site has. *Design, operator decides.*
7. **Fun.** It reads as a CV page. The sundial sweep is subtle and over in 2.6 s. *Design, operator decides.*

## h-crate

**Verdict:** the most njct.io-like page of the four (the crate, the bar, the Bricolage spines) and the only playful idea. Its welcome reads as a caption, and two of the spines carry another product's colours.

1. **The greeting is too small to read as the welcome.** On en.png at (32,1572) "Hi, I'm Neil." is 14 px. It is the smallest text on the screen, under four 60 px brand names. Fix: put the greeting in the site's tagline slot under the header, sized as the site's Details title: add `<h1 class="greeting">` at `position: fixed; inset-inline-start: var(--gutter); top: 60px; font-size: 30px; line-height: 34px; font-weight: 600; letter-spacing: -0.01em;` with the line under it at `14px/18px var(--ink-soft)`. Keep only Save contact in the bar (end-aligned). This also fills the empty band at y 100–340. *Design, operator decides.*
2. **Two spines use the wrong brand colours.** LINE is cyan on teal (the Clauded palette) and WeChat is amber on black (the Chairtax palette), so a visitor from njct.io reads them as those products' covers. A Chinese visitor looks for WeChat green. Fix: WeChat `--paper: #07C160; --title: #FFFFFF`, LINE `--paper: #06C755; --title: #FFFFFF`. If the brand greens look too close side by side, use the site palette with the marks in brand colour instead. *Design, operator decides.*
3. **The order runs backwards to the eye.** The first channel sits just behind Neil, at the bottom of the stack, so the eye reads the last channel first: WeChat on en.png, and LINE at the top on zh.png even though zh leads with WeChat. Fix: `a.style.setProperty('--d', order.length - i)`, which puts the first channel at the top. *Design, operator decides.*
4. **The header is out of line.** "English" sits 2.5 CSS px below "njct.io": text rows 44–69 against 39–64 in en.png, where the site's are 39–65 against 39–64. Fix: `.pm { top: 3px; }`. *Mechanical, builder applies.*
5. **Nothing says the spines can be tapped.** They have no pill, no Open and no underline, and a first-time visitor sees a picture. Fix: give each `.ch-mark` the site's white `rgba(255,255,255,.7)` pill shape at 44 px, or add a hover/press lift (`.ch:active { translate: 0 -6px }`). *Design, operator decides.*
6. **The sphere floats.** Its contact shadow is a thin sliver (en.png at 280,1265), and the ball has a hot spot (`#FFCB80` at 0). Fix: `.sphere-shadow { top: 80%; height: 9%; }` with the core alpha at .7, and start the sphere gradient at `#F0B654`. *Mechanical, builder applies.*
7. **On desktop the shadow is a grey box.** On desk.png at (560–930, 620–1190) the crate shadow shows as a hard rectangle beside the stack. The site uses the shelf there, not the crate. Fix: `@media (min-width: 600px) { .crate-shadow { display: none; } }`. *Mechanical, builder applies.*
8. **The renders don't show the motion.** The rise is skipped in headless renders, so no shot shows it. Before it ships, record one take with the motion on in a non-headless run. *Mechanical, builder applies.*
9. **Fun.** Yes: your contacts as records in Neil's crate is the one idea here someone would show a friend. *Ranking evidence.*

## Ranking

1. **h-crate.** It is the only one a visitor from njct.io would take for the same site at a glance, and the only playful one. Its faults (the tiny greeting, the borrowed colours, the reversed order) can all be fixed without changing the concept.
2. **e-sleeve.** It is faithful and readable, with the clearest hierarchy. Once the LIN glyph, the sun box and the chevron leaves are fixed it is a sound fallback, but it stays a cover above a form.
3. **g-about.** It copies the About page cleanly, but the stock-render ball and the CV tone miss "fun and personal".
4. **f-back.** It has the most AI tells (the centred card with a drop shadow, the blob sun, the glossy marble, the second wordmark, the oversized CTA), and it shrinks the stage-2 head to a 49 px icon. The flip is its only asset.
